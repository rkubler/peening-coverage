Shot Peening Coverage Simulator

The jet sweeps the part at traverse speed V. Each shot leaves a circular dent of radius r. The surface actually covered is measured and compared with the model τ(n) = 100·(1 − e−K·n).

How coverage is calculated

  1. Mean shot massmp = ρ · π E[D³] / 6, number-weighted mean over the size distribution (= ρ π D³/6 if σ = 0)
  2. Shots thrown per secondṄ = ṁ / mp
  3. Impacts per mm² per passR = Ṅ / (V · ΔX): shots per second ÷ area swept per second. A point stays under the jet for ΔX / V = .

  5. Coverage after n passesRandom impacts → a point stays unimpacted with probability e−Kn, hence τ = 100·(1 − e−Kn). 98 % is reached at n = ln 50 / K.
